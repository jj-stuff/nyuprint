Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026. We'll update this page before the app's data practices change, and note the date here.

NYU Print talks to NYU's print portal (Pharos) and NYU's sign-in page, and reads a public announcements file from this website. It has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't collect any data.

Kept on your phone

  • Your NYU sign-in: your NYU email and the print portal's session, stored in the iPhone Keychain. Never your password.
  • Copies of documents you send: so you can preview them and change their options. Deleted when the job leaves your queue.
  • History: a list of jobs you've sent and what happened to them.
  • Preferences: such as map filters.

Sent off your phone

  • To NYU's print portal: documents you choose to print, their print options, and requests to list or delete your jobs.
  • To NYU's sign-in page: you sign in directly on NYU's Microsoft page; the app only receives the resulting session.
  • Announcements: the app downloads announcements.json from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages) to show outage notices and news. The request carries nothing about you; GitHub, like any web host, sees the request's IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Never collected

  • Location: used only on the Stations map to sort nearby printers. Not stored or sent.
  • Usage data: no analytics, crash reporters or advertising identifiers.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account → Privacy → Sign out and erase everything. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Jobs already in your print queue are managed by NYU.
