Support

How do points work?

NYU charges points per side printed: black and white is 3 single-sided or 2 per side double-sided; color is 18 single-sided or 10 per side double-sided. Points are only used when a job prints at a station. Jobs waiting in your queue are shown as "held".

Why does color say "up to"?

Pharos only charges color rates for pages that actually contain color, so a color job usually costs less than the estimate.

Why can't I change a job I sent from my laptop?

The print portal doesn't let apps download queued documents. The app can preview and change jobs it sent itself; jobs from other devices show their details and can be deleted.

Where do I pick up my print?

At any NYU print station: tap your NYU card and release the job. The Stations tab shows every station with floors, hours and directions.

I'm asked to sign in often

On NYU's sign-in page, choose "Yes" on "Stay signed in?" and "Trust this browser" in Duo. The app then reconnects by itself.

Report a bug or send feedback

In the app: Account → Report a bug, or Send feedback. It opens Mail with your app and iOS version filled in.
